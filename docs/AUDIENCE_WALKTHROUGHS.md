# Discuss the purchasing case

Use the same decision in a team meeting or a class. Before showing the timing exhibit, ask the group to explain how the site normally gets the parts and what could go wrong during the wait. [Explore the case](https://safetymp.github.io/Agentic-Process-Safety/#/study)

1. **Establish the problem.** A request for 24 bearings is waiting. What did finance authorize? What does the assembly planner still need to know? Which records could show that a late handoff caused a missed need date?
2. **Predict the release.** Finance approval is current for less than 60 logical seconds. What should happen at 59 seconds? At 60? Change the timing exhibit and explain which rule changes its decision.
3. **Own the consequence.** The order is held. What would you check before buying again? Who will confirm the incoming quantity and receipt date, and who will follow up if stock is still short?
4. **Price the alternatives.** Use the value chart at three and six buyer minutes saved. Explain why the proposal crosses break-even and identify what work the site could actually do with the time freed.
5. **Choose a next step.** Would you keep buyer-led purchasing, add an approval check or run a shadow pilot? Give one reason, the strongest alternative and the evidence that would make you switch.

A group can agree on the approval check and still disagree about funding an agent. A shadow pilot can log how often recommendations differ and how much review time they take. Actual on-time delivery requires receipts matched to material need dates. [Decision worksheet and evidence plan](BUSINESS_CASE.md)

For a usability session, record the build, a reader’s reasoning in their own words, which exhibit they used and any help they needed.
